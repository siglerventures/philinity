Since 2005

A safe, loving home in Visakhapatnam, India

Carunia Children's Home was established in 2005 to give orphaned and at-risk children something every child deserves: a family. Today, children who once faced life alone are growing up surrounded by love — with warm beds, full plates, classrooms to learn in, and caretakers who know each of them by name.

The name Carunia reflects our heart — compassion and grace in action. Every child who walks through our gates is welcomed with the words painted on our wall: prem, asha, vishwas — love, hope, faith.

Our Work

How we care for every child

Whole-child care — body, mind, and spirit — from the day a child arrives until they step into adulthood with confidence.

Nutritious Meals

Three healthy meals every day, prepared with love in our own kitchen. For many children, reliable food is the first step from survival to childhood.

Quality Education

Daily schooling, tutoring, and encouragement to dream big. Our motto is painted on the classroom wall: let your light shine.

A Real Childhood

Safe housing, medical care, play, and the security of a family. Laughter in the courtyard is the sound of healing.

Our Sending Church

A mission of The Living Church, Boise, Idaho

Carunia Children's Home is a nonprofit mission supported through The Living Church in Boise, Idaho. Across two continents, one church family prays for, gives to, and visits the children of Carunia — a living partnership between Boise and Visakhapatnam.

Every dollar given goes directly to the care of the children: food, schooling, clothing, medical care, and the faithful staff who love them daily.
